## 摘要
长期以来，俄歇电子能谱一直与X射线光电子能谱相伴，作为第二种模态来分辨具有重叠核心电子结合能的化学态。然而，俄歇能谱的分析受到其复杂性以及模拟计算成本高的挑战。在这里，我们证明，核心空穴的产生与其相应的俄歇-迈特纳衰变之间的物理联系，因而任务间关系，使得通过训练多任务图神经网络能够实现归纳知识迁移，从而从共同的图嵌入预测这两个观测量。两个任务的损失通过不确定性加权方法用学习到的权重进行组合。总体而言，单任务和多任务模型在大多数情况下都能以良好的精度预测计算的和实验的俄歇谱线形状。两种任务设置之间的性能相似，而单任务模型通常对能谱中更精细的峰结构有更好的预测。目前的结果表明，多任务训练是未来发展通用X射线光谱模型的一条有前景的途径，这些模型具有学习到的表示，可将分子结构映射到多种技术。

## Abstract
Auger-electron spectroscopy has long accompanied x-ray photoelectron spectroscopy as a second modality to resolve chemical states with overlapping core-electron binding energies. However, analyzing the Auger spectrum is challenged by its complexity and the computational expense of its simulation. Here we demonstrate that the physical connection, and thus inter-task relationship, between the generation of a core-hole and its corresponding Auger-Meitner decay enables inductive knowledge transfer through the training of a multi-task graph neural network to predict both observables from a common graph embedding. Both task losses are combined with learned weights via the uncertainty weighting procedure. Overall, the single-task and multi-task models predict calculated and experimental Auger lineshapes with good accuracy in most cases. The performance between the two task regimes is similar, with the single-task models generally having better predictions of the finer peak structures in the spectrum. The present results demonstrate that multi-task training is a promising avenue for future developments of universal x-ray spectroscopy models with learned representations that map the molecular structure to a multitude of techniques.
