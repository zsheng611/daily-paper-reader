## 摘要
对于原子尺度分子动力学模拟，我们考虑最近提出的一种混合耦合方法，它将高精度的基于机器学习（ML）的原子团簇展开（ACE）相互作用模型与精度较低（但速度约快1-2个数量级）的EAM势耦合起来，以应对纯ML势在并行计算环境中的性能瓶颈。这种时空自适应性具有实现超过一个数量级加速的潜力，但如果没有动态负载均衡策略，这一潜力将会丧失；由于势贡献波动，动态负载均衡变得至关重要。我们在铜纳米压痕的自适应精度EAM-ACE模拟中，将LAMMPS的负载均衡策略与ALL库提供的负载均衡方法进行比较。对于规则网格区域分解，ALL库的改进张量方法保持了张量方法的简单性和可迁移性，与LAMMPS原生的负载均衡方法相比，它将力计算的不平衡因子从3.0显著降低到1.7。通过使用LAMMPS的递归二分法对不规则区域网格进行负载均衡，以及使用ALL的直方图方法对交错区域网格进行负载均衡，可以达到不平衡因子接近1.0的近乎完美均衡系统。研究发现，使用ALL对交错网格进行负载均衡明显快于使用LAMMPS的二分法对不规则网格进行负载均衡，但在常规时间步中，不规则区域网格的通信成本更低，因为其区域表面积与体积之比更低。此外，我们提出了一种为自适应精度模拟寻找理想负载均衡频率的策略。

## Abstract
For atomistic molecular dynamics simulations, we consider a recently developed hybrid coupling between the highly accurate machine learning (ML)-based atomic cluster expansion (ACE) interaction model and a less precise (but about 1-2 orders faster) EAM potential, in order to leverage the performance bottleneck of pure ML potentials in a parallel computing environment. This spatial-temporal adaptivity has the potential for a speedup of more than an order of magnitude, that would be lost without a dynamic load-balancing strategy that becomes critical due to fluctuating potential contributions. We compare the load-balancing strategies of LAMMPS with load-balancing methods provided by the library ALL in adaptive-precision EAM-ACE simulations of a copper nanoindentation. For a regular-grid domain decomposition, the modified tensor method of the ALL library, which conserves the simplicity and transferability of the tensor method, significantly reduces the imbalance factor of the force calculation from 3.0 to 1.7 compared to the native load-balancing method of LAMMPS. A nearly perfectly balanced system with an imbalance factor close to 1.0 can be reached by load balancing an irregular grid of domains with the recursive bisectioning method of LAMMPS and with ALL's histogram method for a staggered grid of domains. Load balancing a staggered grid with ALL is found to be significantly faster than load balancing an irregular grid with the bisectioning method of LAMMPS, but the communication costs during a regular timestep are lower for the irregular grid of domains, as a lower surface-by-volume ratio of the domains is reached. Furthermore, we suggest a strategy to find the ideal load-balancing frequency for adaptive-precision simulations.
