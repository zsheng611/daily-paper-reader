## 摘要
预测结构复杂材料中的介电与压电响应需要大的模拟晶胞，而密度泛函微扰理论（DFPT）计算随 $\mathcal{O}(N^4)$ 标度，变得在计算上难以承受。然而，机器学习方法仍然具有挑战性，要么受限于直接回归所需的大量昂贵 DFPT 数据，要么受限于重构的立方标度成本。我们在此提出一个机器学习框架，将离子响应学习为按原子索引的场：DART（Direct Atom-Resolved response-Tensor learning，直接原子分辨响应张量学习）直接学习这些场，并能从小训练集获得高精度；而 LARS（Linear-scaling Atom-Resolved Response Solver，线性标度原子分辨响应求解器）通过稀疏线性求解，从学到的微观组分重构这些场，并且对于离子介电或压电张量不需要 DFPT 标签。利用 DART，我们外推到训练中未出现的 314 种 AlN/ScN 超晶格堆叠，并识别出一个高响应极性候选，DFPT 给出横向钳制压电应变系数 $d_{33,f}=14.511$ pC/N 和 $k_t^2=20.46\%$，比有序 1AlN/1ScN 参考分别高 63% 和 66%。

## Abstract
Predicting dielectric and piezoelectric responses in structurally complex materials requires large simulation cells, for which density-functional perturbation theory (DFPT) calculations scale as $\mathcal{O}(N^4)$ and become computationally prohibitive. However, machine-learning approaches have remained challenging, limited either by the large amounts of expensive DFPT data required for direct regression or by the cubic cost of reconstruction. We here propose a machine-learning framework that learns the ionic response as an atom-indexed field: DART (Direct Atom-Resolved response-Tensor learning) learns these fields directly and achieves high accuracy from small training sets, whereas LARS (Linear-scaling Atom-Resolved Response Solver) reconstructs them from learned microscopic ingredients through sparse linear solves and requires no DFPT labels for the ionic dielectric or piezoelectric tensors. Using DART, we extrapolate to 314 stackings of AlN/ScN superlattices absent from training and identify a high-response polar candidate, for which DFPT gives a laterally clamped piezoelectric strain coefficient $d_{33,f}=14.511$ pC/N and $k_t^2=20.46\%$, exceeding the ordered 1AlN/1ScN reference by 63% and 66%.
