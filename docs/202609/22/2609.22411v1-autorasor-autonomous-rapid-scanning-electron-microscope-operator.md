## 摘要
扫描电子显微镜（SEM）是表征材料微观结构的基础技术，而材料微观结构决定了许多基本物理和化学性质。随着自驱动实验室（SDL）的兴起，样品现在可以大批量合成，这就要求同样高通量、自主化的表征。现有的自动化电子显微镜流程通常是任务特定的：它们检测预定义特征、优化已知性质，或需要关于样品的先验知识，从而将每个流程限制在其所针对的材料体系内。我们提出 AutoRASOR，一种与任务无关的自主 SEM 流程，无需领域特定的预训练、微调或人工提示，即可捕获未知样品的多尺度形貌。AutoRASOR 利用视觉基础模型（VFM）DINOv3 实时嵌入显微图像，并通过两种互补策略选择感兴趣区域（ROI）：潜在最远点采样（LFPS）和基于形态模糊性的主动学习，后者是在给定低倍率外观条件下未解析细尺度特征的条件方差。基于模糊性的主动学习比随机 ROI 选择能持续捕获更多样和稀有的形态，而 LFPS 能在有限采集预算内可靠地恢复样本的形态分布；这一点在真实 SEM 显微图像和合成相场图像上均得到验证。通过将自主表征从预定义的、任务特定的特征靶向转变为形态调查，AutoRASOR 能为各种下游任务生成信息丰富的多尺度数据集。无需领域特定预训练或材料先验假设，该框架可以开箱即用地部署，以表征由 SDL 生产的新材料。

## Abstract
Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM) is a foundational technique for characterizing material microstructure, which dictates many fundamental physical and chemical properties. With the rise of Self-Driving Labs (SDLs), samples are now synthesized in large batches, demanding equally high-throughput, autonomous characterization. Existing automated electron microscopy pipelines are task-specific: they detect pre-defined features, optimize known properties, or require prior knowledge of the sample, restricting each pipeline to the material system it was built for. We introduce AutoRASOR, a task-agnostic autonomous SEM pipeline that captures the multi-scale morphology of an unknown sample without domain-specific pre-training, fine-tuning, or human prompting. AutoRASOR embeds micrographs in real time with a vision foundation model (VFM), DINOv3, and selects regions of interest (ROIs) through two complementary policies: Latent Farthest Point Sampling (LFPS) and active learning on morphological ambiguity, which is the conditional variance of unresolved fine-scale features given lower magnification appearance. Active learning on ambiguity consistently captures more diverse and rare morphologies than random ROI selection, while LFPS reliably recovers the specimen's morphological distribution within a limited capture budget, tested both on real SEM micrographs and synthetic phase-field images. By shifting autonomous characterization from pre-defined, task-specific targeting of features to morphological survey, AutoRASOR generates information-rich multi-scale datasets for diverse downstream tasks. Without the need for domain-specific pre-training or prior material assumptions, this framework can be deployed out-of-the-box to characterize novel materials produced by SDLs.
