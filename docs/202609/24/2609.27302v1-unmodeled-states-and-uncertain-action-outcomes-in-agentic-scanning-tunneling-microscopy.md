## 摘要
在物理实验中，干预可能会以无法预先预测的方式改变隐藏的实验状态。因此，自主科学智能体必须在实验状态知识不完整的情况下运行，同时解读其干预的后果。本文利用扫描隧道显微镜（STM）针尖处理来研究这一问题，该任务传统上依赖人类专家，且受难以获取的针尖尖端条件与不确定的动作结果支配。我们提出 quailbot，一种智能体执行框架，通过将物理干预与实验回读关联，使 LLM 处于仪器反馈回路中。在经过人类专家的简短学徒期后，前沿 LLM 智能体自主完成了 STM 上的端到端针尖处理，并通过独立验证。实验中出现的意外情况进一步暴露了当相关实验状态或动力学隐藏或未建模时，LLM 智能体自主性的局限。我们的结果表明，未来的自主物理实验要求智能体推断隐藏和未建模的实验状态，追踪其动作结果，并在真实仪器的可观测性与动作限制内运行。

## Abstract
In physical experiments, interventions can alter hidden experimental states in ways that cannot be predicted in advance. Autonomous scientific agents must therefore interpret the consequences of their interventions while operating with incomplete knowledge of the experimental state. Here we investigate this problem using scanning tunneling microscopy (STM) tip conditioning, traditionally a human-expert-intensive task governed by inaccessible tip apex conditions and uncertain action outcomes. We introduce quailbot, an agent harness that places the LLM inside the instrument feedback loop by linking physical interventions with experimental readbacks. After a brief apprenticeship with a human expert, frontier LLM agents autonomously completed end-to-end tip conditioning on an STM and passed an independent verification. The contingencies that arose during the experiments further exposed the limits of LLM agentic autonomy when relevant experimental states or dynamics were hidden or unmodeled. Our results show that future autonomous physical experimentation requires agents to infer hidden and unmodeled experimental states, track the outcomes of their actions, and operate within the observability and action limits of real instruments.
